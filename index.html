AIOS App · Privacy Policy

Operated by YUN GLOBAL HOLDINGS LLC · Effective date: October 19, 2026

Who we are

AIOS App is an internal business tool operated by YUN GLOBAL HOLDINGS LLC ("we", "us"). We use it only to manage the advertising accounts, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts that belong to our own business and brands. It is not offered to the public and it does not manage accounts of third parties.

What data the app accesses

  • Configuration and performance data of our own advertising accounts through the Meta Marketing API: campaigns, ad sets, ads, budgets, spend and results.
  • Content, comments and messages received on our own Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, so that we can answer the people who contact our brands.

We do not access data from advertising accounts, Pages or Instagram accounts that do not belong to us.

How we use the data

  • To create, run, measure and adjust our own advertising.
  • To prepare internal reports about the results of our business.
  • To reply to comments and messages from people who contact our brands.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent any data. We only share data with service providers that process it on our behalf, such as cloud hosting and AI processing providers, under confidentiality obligations and only for the purposes above, or when the law requires it.

Retention and security

We keep data only as long as it is needed for the purposes above, and then we delete it. Access is limited to the owners of the business, and the credentials of the app are stored securely and never shared.

Your choices and data deletion

If you contacted one of our brands and want us to delete the data related to you, write to hola@udamia.com with the subject "Data deletion". We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly process data of children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Questions: hola@udamia.com.

AIOS App · Política de privacidad

Operada por YUN GLOBAL HOLDINGS LLC · Vigente desde el 19 de octubre de 2026

Quiénes somos

AIOS App es una herramienta interna de YUN GLOBAL HOLDINGS LLC. La usamos solo para administrar las cuentas publicitarias, las páginas de Facebook y las cuentas de Instagram de nuestro propio negocio y nuestras marcas. No se ofrece al público y no administra cuentas de terceros.

Qué datos usa

  • La configuración y los resultados de nuestras propias cuentas publicitarias, por la API de Marketing de Meta: campañas, conjuntos de anuncios, anuncios, presupuestos, gasto y resultados.
  • El contenido, los comentarios y los mensajes que reciben nuestras propias páginas y cuentas de Instagram, para responder a quienes se comunican con nuestras marcas.

Para qué

Para crear, medir y ajustar nuestra publicidad, para nuestros informes internos y para responder comentarios y mensajes. No vendemos ni alquilamos datos. Solo los comparten proveedores que los procesan por cuenta nuestra (alojamiento en la nube y procesamiento con inteligencia artificial), con confidencialidad y para estos fines, o cuando lo exige la ley.

Plazo, seguridad y borrado

Guardamos los datos solo mientras hacen falta para estos fines y después los borramos. Para pedir el borrado de tus datos, escribe a hola@udamia.com con el asunto «Borrado de datos»: lo confirmamos y lo hacemos dentro de los 30 días.
